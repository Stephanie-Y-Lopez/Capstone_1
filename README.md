## Hello all and welcome to the South Carolina analytical report for EmporiUm student bookstore.
The tools I have used to complete this project consist of, MySQL to query, Excel to demonstrate the data on charts, GitBash to locally commit and GitHub to commit to the cloud to track and update my work.
# Territory 
I have been assigned to South Carolina and my regional range consist of South Carolina, Florida and Texas.
# What do my queries consist of?
This analytical report provides an overview of sales performance in South Carolina. It examines total revenue, yearly and monthly revenue breakdowns, and compares South Carolina's revenue to its overall region. It also analyzes monthly transaction counts and average transaction amounts by product category, providing online sales information, and a recommendation for improving sales for next months report. 
# In this repository I have uploaded,
- The MYSQL queries to answer statical progress report questions.
- As well as Excel Pivotcharts and bar charts for what the revenue break down by year and month, along with the number of transactions per month and average transactions amount based on a products category for the South territory.
